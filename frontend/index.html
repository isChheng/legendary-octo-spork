How activity is measured

The calendar counts each 0.1° grid cell once per UTC day when it contains a nominal- or high-confidence detection. Repeated passes and detections from multiple instruments in that cell share the same count. Known non-vegetation hotspots are excluded. Detections with no hotspot type remain eligible.

Active cell-days are a shared activity index. Grid-cell area varies with latitude. Comparisons across months are adjusted for the satellites flying at the time, using factors measured where the satellites overlap; the adjustment is statistical and most reliable for large areas and whole months. This measure cannot identify individual fires or burned area.

The comparison uses up to five previous years for the same month and requires matching satellite coverage and at least two complete historical months. Coverage is inferred from monthly observation dates. Days without an activity bar have no qualifying records. Hatched months in History have no qualifying records.

The map groups NASA FIRMS MODIS and VIIRS detections into a latitude–longitude grid for the selected dates and satellite record. At wide zoom levels, dots mark occupied cells. Zoom in to see smaller grid cells. Brighter marks contain more detections. Select a mark to see its detection count, days with detections, platforms, and summed fire radiative power (FRP) in megawatts. The Hotspots CSV contains individual detections. The dark map uses Natural Earth and geoBoundaries geography, and the satellite basemap adds NASA Blue Marble imagery.
